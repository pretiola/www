01 / How we help

Good intentions deserve
practical follow-through.

Start with the problem. Choose the simplest approach the people using it can sustain. Make the result clear enough to hand over.

I

Digital stewardship

Reliable websites, clear information and maintainable tools. Technology serves the people responsible for the mission.

A useful starting point: a website review and a prioritized improvement brief.

II

Planning & accountability

Clear priorities, agreed responsibilities and useful records. Turn an intention into a manageable piece of work.

A useful starting point: a scoped plan with an owner and a definition of done.

III

Evidence & communication

Explain what happened, with permission and context. Keep completed work distinct from what is still being planned.

A useful starting point: a clear, attributable project update.

These are starting points for a conversation. Scope and availability are agreed case by case.

02 / How participation works

One useful piece of work.
Done together.

A small commitment, clearly understood, can be a meaningful place to start.

Find your way to contribute →

  1. Start with a conversation

    Share the need, the experience you bring and the time you can realistically give.

  2. Agree a manageable scope

    Name the result, coordinator, support and time boundary before anyone commits.

  3. Work alongside the people responsible

    Local knowledge and decision-making stay with the mission. Expertise contributes to that work.

  4. Review the result

    Hand over what was agreed, learn from the experience and decide together what comes next.

04 / Why Pretiola exists

Experience has more
to give.

A place for practical experience in works of charity—and for learning through service alongside others.

Pretiola is an independent initiative of lay Catholics. It is not an official body of the Holy See or a diocese. Faith shapes the invitation; care, clarity and responsibility should shape the work.
