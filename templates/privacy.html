About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pretiola Initiative (“Pretiola”, “we”, “us”) collects and uses personal information through this website and its inquiry forms. For privacy questions or requests, contact contact@pretiola.org.

Information we collect

When you submit an inquiry, we collect your name, email address, message and consent to be contacted. Contributor inquiries also include your selected contribution category and any availability, time zone, interests or referral information you choose to provide. Ministry inquiries include the organization name and country.

We also process limited technical information to operate and protect the forms, including a security cookie and information used to detect repeated submissions. Hosting services may process connection information, including IP addresses, in their own operational logs.

Do not submit passwords, payment details, identity documents, health information or confidential information about other people. Only provide information you are authorized to share.

How we use information

We use your information to review and respond to inquiries, discuss potential contributions or ministry support, maintain records of those conversations, and protect the website against spam and misuse. We do not use inquiry information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

Access and service providers

Inquiry records are stored by the Pretiola application and are available through restricted administrative access. Access is intended for people responsible for reviewing and handling inquiries. Our production hosting configuration uses Fly.io and persistent database storage. Hosting, messaging, email and backup providers may process information as necessary to supply their services.

When Telegram delivery is enabled, submitted contact details, messages and other inquiry fields are sent through Telegram to designated reviewers. Email notifications, when used instead, contain only an inquiry reference and category. We may disclose information where required by applicable law or where necessary to address unlawful activity or protect legal rights.

Cookies and analytics

The website uses an essential cookie named pretiola_form to protect form submissions. It expires after up to 24 hours and is not used for advertising. If you disable cookies, you can contact us by email instead of submitting a form.

We use temporarily processed network addresses and keyed hashes of network and email addresses to limit repeated submissions. Raw network addresses are not stored in inquiry records. The website does not currently use analytics or advertising trackers.

Data retention

Inquiry records and associated notification jobs are automatically removed from the application after 90 days. This automatic deletion does not remove copies delivered to Telegram; those copies require separate deletion by the responsible reviewers. Separate records created for an agreed engagement are outside this inquiry process and require their own retention arrangements.

Deleted information may remain in backups until those backups expire. If a backup is restored, previously approved deletion requests must be reapplied.

Security

We use access restrictions, submission validation and abuse controls to protect inquiry information. No system or method of transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your inquiry information, or to ask us to stop contacting you. Include your inquiry reference or the email address used for the inquiry. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy of information, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or complain to the relevant data protection authority. These rights may be subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

Third-party websites

Links to external websites are provided for convenience. This policy does not apply to those websites. Review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the website or our information-handling practices. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your personal information, email contact@pretiola.org.
