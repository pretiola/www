Who to contact

The website introduces the Pretiola Initiative. For questions about an inquiry or your information, contact contact@pretiola.org.

What the inquiry forms collect

Both forms ask for your name, reply email, message and permission to reply. Ministry inquiries also ask for an organization and country. Contributor introductions ask for a preferred contribution mode, with optional availability, time zone and interests. A referral is optional.

Please do not include identity documents, passwords, payment details, medical information or sensitive information about other people. We do not need those details to discuss an initial inquiry.

What happens when you submit

The form sends your information to the Pretiola application, which validates and saves the inquiry before confirming receipt. The revised form does not send submissions to Web3Forms.

In the production configuration, the application uses Fly.io hosting and a persistent database volume. A notification tells the designated recipient that an inquiry is available; the notification contains a reference and inquiry type, not your submitted contact details or message. Authorized operators retrieve the record through restricted administrative access.

We use inquiry information to assess the request, reply and record its progress. An inquiry does not guarantee a placement, engagement or response time.

Essential form cookie and abuse prevention

A cookie called pretiola_form helps confirm that a submission came from the form you opened. It lasts up to 24 hours and is used for form security, not advertising. Cookies must be enabled to submit; you can email us instead.

The application uses your network address temporarily to limit repeated requests. Saved rate-limit keys use keyed hashes of network addresses and email addresses; the application does not save raw network addresses in inquiry records. Hosting infrastructure may separately process connection information.

Retention and deletion

The application removes inquiry records and their notification jobs after 90 days. If we agree to work together, any separate engagement records and their retention need to be explained separately.

You can ask us to locate, correct or delete an inquiry using your reply address or inquiry reference. We may need to confirm that the request comes from the person concerned. Backups can retain a copy until their retention period ends; deletion requests must also be respected if a backup is restored.
